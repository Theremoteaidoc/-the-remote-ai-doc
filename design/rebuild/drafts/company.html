Dr. Javier Rosas, MD

Founder & CEO

Leadership

Dr. Javier Rosas is a Senior Ship Physician with a major cruise line, where he oversees clinical practice in remote environments often thousands of miles from shore medical facilities. His experience in occupational and maritime medicine led to the founding of RemoteAid Medical Solutions, Corp.

SeaScope exists to bridge the gap between ship and shore. By applying structured clinical review to the pre-employment medical and televideo consults at sea, we provide fleet operators with the documented duty of care required in modern global shipping.
